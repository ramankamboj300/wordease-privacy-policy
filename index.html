Privacy Policy

WordEase: Word Search Puzzle · Effective date: 10 November 2026

In short: WordEase does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your game progress stays on your device. To show ads and understand how the game is played, the app uses Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase, which collect some device information as described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how the WordEase mobile app ("WordEase", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise (GPS) location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so you can continue playing. It is not sent to us:

  • Game progress, levels completed, stars and coins
  • Daily puzzle streak and calendar
  • Settings such as text size, sound, language and input mode

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may automatically collect information from your device under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhat it may collectWhy
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app version, ad interactions, approximate location (from IP) To show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud
Google Analytics for Firebase App instance ID, device model, operating system, language, country, app version and gameplay events (for example: level started, level completed, hint used, time spent on a level) To understand how the game is played, fix problems and balance level difficulty

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy

4. Ads and your choices

  • Ads in WordEase are set to be suitable for general audiences.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices (ads).
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • You can play the game without an internet connection; no ads or analytics are sent while offline.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove Ads" are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your card or payment details.

6. Data sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, so they can provide their services, or when required by law. We do not sell or rent your information.

7. Data retention and security

Information on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google services is kept according to Google's retention policies. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit.

8. Children's privacy

WordEase is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in Europe or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold your name or contact details, most data can be removed by uninstalling the app and resetting your Advertising ID. For any request, email us at the address below.
